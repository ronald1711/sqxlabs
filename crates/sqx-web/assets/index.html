Before relying on a candidate

Use a saved dataset and explicit account settings. Register the search and keep final holdout data untouched until selection is complete. Inspect walk-forward and stress-test results, then explain every difference in an independent execution comparison. Treat missing evidence as unknown.

Settings

        

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        

        
One SqxLabs point is one FX pip: 0.0001 for EURUSD and 0.01 for USDJPY. For a broker quoting five or three decimals, 10 broker points equal 1 pip. Example: one USDJPY broker point of 0.001 means entering 0.1 here. Slippage applies to each fill; commission is charged once per completed trade. Tick backtests use observed bid/ask spread.

        
Off by default. Inspect the dataset and calendar first. Acknowledging gaps does not repair missing data or validate the result.

        
Candle execution assumption

        
        
        
Candles do not reveal the actual price sequence. Reports flag candles where the two OHLC paths differ. These scenarios are not mathematical worst/best bounds.

        
        
Account and broker assumptions

        

          
          
          
          
          
        

        
These are simulation assumptions, not a broker specification. Financing is charged at UTC day boundaries; negative values are credits. Zero means financing is excluded.
